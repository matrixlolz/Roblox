05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you acquire Roblox games?

Yes. If your game has strong retention but you lack the team to scale it, reach out. We offer upfront payments, revenue share, or both.

How much does a brand world cost?

It depends on scope. Most projects take 8–16 weeks. Tell us your goals and we'll send a proposal within a few days.

Can I pitch you a game idea?

Ideas are cheap; prototypes aren't. Send us something playable, even if it's rough, and we'll take a look.

Where is the team based?

Everywhere. We're remote-first with people across Europe, the Americas, and Asia, and we meet up twice a year.
